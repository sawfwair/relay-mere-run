# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and acknowledge mistakes.
- Keep technical discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Protect private information shared during security or conduct reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, and other
spaces where someone represents the project. Report conduct concerns privately
to `hello@sawfwair.com`. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or
disruptive. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
